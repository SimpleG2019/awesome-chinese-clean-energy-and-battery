## 🔬 Core Focus Areas
- 🔋 **Solid-State & Sodium-Ion Batteries**: Anode/cathode interfaces, sulfide/oxide solid electrolytes, thermal runaway suppression.
- ☀️ **Perovskite & Tandem Solar PV**: Record power conversion efficiencies, moisture degradation mitigation, scalable manufacturing.
- ⚡ **Ultra-High Voltage (UHV) Smart Grids**: Multi-terminal HVDC transmission, grid-scale BESS, real-time load dispatch algorithms.
- 🌱 **Green Hydrogen & Carbon Capture (CCUS)**: PEM & alkaline water electrolysis catalysts, geological carbon sequestration.
